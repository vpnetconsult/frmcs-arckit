# Project charter — Agentic AI autonomous oversight (FRMCS)

**Delivery party:** Vpnet Cloud Solutions Sdn. Bhd. · sales@vpnet.cloud · Co. No. 1650064-D (Malaysia)
**Status:** Draft · **Amended 2026-08-19 — standing corrected, two-track structure adopted**
**Original:** 2026-06-24 · **Method:** arcKit — outcome-anchored, ADR-recorded, evidence-logged, daily-baselined

> **Amendment note (2026-08-19).** The original charter named a **"Client: Infrastructure Manager (IM) — to be named"** and set **"safety case accepted by the NSA"** as a success criterion. **Neither was ever true.** No IM commissioned this work, no National Safety Authority has any relationship to it, and none was ever going to accept a safety case for it in this form. The register carried that framing for eight weeks and 355 evidence rows. This amendment corrects it. **Nothing in the evidence base changes — evidence is evidence regardless of who asked for it — but what the register may claim about its own standing changes completely.** See §"What this charter is not".

## Outcome (the anchor — unchanged)

Safe, continuous rail operations through **governed** autonomy. Every workstream, agent and gate exists to serve this; nothing is built that does not trace to it.

## Standing — read this before anything else

**There is no client.** This work is an **independent shadow assessment**, built by Vpnet from **publicly available, tier-graded, verified evidence**. It is not commissioned, not mandated, not funded by, and not endorsed by any infrastructure manager, railway undertaking, safety authority, standards body or vendor named anywhere in this register.

Every organisation appearing in these files — DB InfraGO, EBA, DZSF, ERA, ERJU, BSI, the vendors — appears as a **subject of analysis or a source of evidence**, never as a party to an engagement. Where an artefact is addressed *to* such a body (the EBA consultation note, the ACS enquiry), it is an **unsolicited approach from an independent party** and must state that on its face.

## Two tracks

The engagement has one method and two distinct purposes. Confusing them is the failure mode this amendment exists to prevent.

### Track A — EU/D: the evidence base

**What it is.** A shadow architecture assessment of the GSM-R → FRMCS transition and of agentic AI as an oversight layer, developed against Germany and the EU because that is **the most thoroughly documented rail-digitalisation programme in the world** — its regulators, research institute and standardisation bodies publish their reasoning, which makes the argument falsifiable by anyone who cares to check.

**Why Germany specifically.** The 23–24 June 2026 GSM-R outage supplies an empirical anchor no synthetic case could match: a real silent-failover fault on a live national safety-carrying estate, with the operator's own published post-incident account. The EU legal layer (AI Act, CRA, NIS-2, RED, CSM-RA, TSIs) supplies a **worked regulatory reasoning** that exists nowhere else at this resolution.

**Conclusion route — the assessment closes on its own evidence.** The deliverable is an **evidence-based assessment that stands on its own sourcing**: every load-bearing claim traceable to a tier-graded row, falsifiable by anyone who checks the same public record. **The conclusion does not wait on anyone's blessing** and is not contingent on a response from any body.

**Outreach is permitted — the test is what is being asked for, not when.**

| | Peer / community exchange — **in scope** | Approval-seeking — **out of scope, permanently** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Sharing findings, contributing analysis, practitioner dialogue, participating in a network Vpnet already belongs to | Consultation, concurrence, ratification, endorsement, "confirm our decision is correct" |
| Standing claimed | Independent party with something useful to contribute | A mandate, a client relationship, or a pending authorisation — **none of which exist** |
| Model | **The BSI *Allianz für Cyber-Sicherheit* membership** — a real, held membership in a **cooperation and information-sharing network**: reciprocal, no authority claimed on either side | The `NSA concurrence outstanding` framing — a validation this work is not seeking and could not obtain |
| Reciprocity | Vpnet gives findings and receives material and correction | Vpnet asks a body to underwrite a position it never commissioned |

**Why the distinction is load-bearing.** Approval-seeking is excluded **not for timing reasons but because it misrepresents standing** — and because the approval is unavailable in any case. Peer exchange carries no such defect: it claims nothing that is not true. A finding shared into the ACS is a contribution from a member; the same finding sent to a safety authority under a request for concurrence invites the reader to infer a mandate that does not exist.

**Feedback arriving through peer exchange is evidence and is logged as such**, tiered honestly on attribution and expertise — a named practitioner's correction is not tier A because it is inconvenient to dismiss, nor tier D because it arrived informally. Corrections that land are applied to the ADRs, not defended against.

**Stakeholder identification is in scope and continues, independently of any outreach.**
`05-stakeholders.md` and the named-contact rows (e.g. E-2026-08-18-08) are **analytical products built from public statements** — who holds which mandate, who publishes what, who decides what. **Naming a party is not approaching them.** The mapping serves two purposes, both live:

1. **Readiness.** When findings are shared or presented, Vpnet knows which party holds which mandate and can address the right one on the merits.
2. **Role-equivalence — the actual bridge to Track B.** The map's value is not the names; it is the **function set**: safety authority, safety-research institute, standardisation body, sector coordinator, infrastructure manager, spectrum regulator, cyber authority, independent assessor, notified body, funding ministry. **That function set is the template for locating the Malaysian equivalents** — and mapping who occupies each role in Malaysia is a Track B deliverable, derived from this one.

**Where that leaves the two drafted approaches.**
- **`09-acs-enquiry-2026-08-18.md` — consistent with the model and may proceed.** It runs through a membership Vpnet actually holds, and its own opening checks already state the correct standing: the ACS "is not a submission channel", creates no regulatory interface, and does **not** discharge ADR-012 action 9. That is peer exchange, correctly framed.
- **`08-eba-consultation-2026-08-18.md` — held, and requires reframing before it could ever go.** Its stated purpose is to "discharge the NSA consultation" and "clear the `NSA concurrence outstanding` qualifier" — **approval-shaped, and therefore out of scope as drafted.** A letter sharing findings with the EBA on their merits would be a different document with a different opening, and is a decision to take on its own terms.

**Success for Track A is falsification-resistance, not approval.** No EU body will ever ratify this work, and the charter no longer pretends otherwise.

### Track B — Malaysia: the deployment

**What it is.** Conversion of the validated framework into **arcKit-governed deliverables for a real Malaysian engagement**, with **Vpnet Cloud Solutions Sdn. Bhd. as the local entity** actively engaging **U Mobile (EIP platform)**, delivery partners and government counterparties on mission-critical communications and governed autonomy — **leveraging the EU/D knowledge base as method and precedent, not as applicable law.**

**First deliverable — the Malaysian stakeholder map, by role-equivalence.** Take the Track A function set (safety authority · safety-research body · standardisation body · sector coordinator · infrastructure manager / operator · spectrum regulator · cyber authority · independent assessor · funding ministry) and establish **who occupies each role in Malaysia**, from primary domestic sources, to the evidentiary standard Track A applies to EU law. **This is where the EU/D work pays out**: eight weeks of watching how a mature regime distributes these mandates is what makes it possible to ask the right question of the right Malaysian body first time.

**Status: forming.** This track has **no evidence rows in the register as of 2026-08-19** — zero on U Mobile, zero on the Malaysian regulatory layer, zero on the domestic rail or critical-comms estate. **Track B is currently an intention with a strong methodology behind it and no evidence base of its own.** That asymmetry is the honest position and must not be papered over: 355 rows of German evidence do not constitute evidence about Malaysia.

**The transfer basis is itself a decision and is owed an ADR.** What crosses from Track A to Track B is **method, reasoning and precedent** — the arcKit discipline itself, CENELEC practice, the silent-failover lesson, the oversight-not-control guardrail, the safety/security adjudication problem (ADR-012 item 9), the CSM-RA "no code of practice → explicit risk estimation" *reasoning*. What does **not** cross is the **EU legal spine**: Reg (EU) 2024/1689 (on which ADR-003 wholly rests), the CRA, NIS-2, the RED, TSI/ERA authorisation, and the ECC-harmonised 1900 MHz n101 spectrum premise in ADR-001. **Deployment jurisdiction and market placement are separate questions** — EU instruments retain extraterritorial reach for anything later placed on the EU market. The Malaysian regulatory layer must be built from primary Malaysian sources and is a **deliverable of Track B, not an assumption of it**.

## Mandate (what Vpnet is actually doing)

Design and assure the agentic decision & oversight layer specified in ADR-002 — the decision plane and runtime plane — over an FRMCS-class bearer, under human oversight bounded by decision class; **evidence it against the EU/D corpus (Track A) and deliver it into a Malaysian engagement (Track B)**.

## Scope

**In scope**
- The two agent planes (Risk Sentinel, Decision, Bid/RFP, Assurance; Anomaly, Resilience, Intent).
- The human-in-the-loop gate and its decision-class routing.
- The evidence chain, eval harness, and AI management system (NIST AI RMF / ISO 42001).
- Integration to an FRMCS-class bearer and to operator telemetry/incident sources.
- **Track A:** the assessment, its evidence base, its stakeholder *identification* (mapping mandates from public sources), and its publication at conclusion.
- **Track B:** the Malaysian stakeholder map by role-equivalence, the domestic governance layer, counterparty engagement, and localised deliverables.

**Out of scope (hard boundary)**
- **Autonomous safety actuation.** The system watches and proposes; it never commands movement authorities, emergency stop, or degraded-mode entry autonomously. Those stay human-in-command.
- The certified SIL-4 safety kernel itself (the operator's / signalling supplier's certified asset).
- The FRMCS RAN/core build (vendor prime under ADR-001).
- **Approval-, concurrence- or endorsement-seeking approaches to any body.** Sharing findings as an independent contributor is in scope; asking anyone to underwrite the work is not, at any point.
- **Any representation that this work is commissioned, mandated or endorsed by a party that has not said so in writing.**

## Success criteria

**Track A — the assessment holds up**
- The assessment **concludes** — a stated position on each requirement, resting on the evidence base, with its limits declared. Not left permanently open because one more source might arrive, and not contingent on any body's response.
- The argument is **falsifiable and self-contained**: every load-bearing claim traceable to a tier-graded row, checkable by a reader with no access we do not also have.
- The **stakeholder function map is complete enough to be transferable** — each mandate identified and sourced, ready to be role-matched in Malaysia.
- The evidence base stays honest: trust tiers accurate, claims paraphrased not pasted, every row answering the decision question.
- The register's own failure mode stays visible and treated — revise rate and ratification counters read at every baseline, not audited into existence after the fact.
- Incident-replay of the 23–24 Jun outage demonstrates, on the evidence, that a ~2 h standstill was reducible to a supervised, minutes-long degraded mode (R3/R4).
- Risk Sentinel raises decision-ready alerts before a threshold is crossed (closes the awareness gap, R12).
- Every agent decision class maps to a named human role; safety-critical actuation is provably human-in-command (R10/R11).

**Track B — the framework converts**
- A Malaysian regulatory and standards layer established **from primary domestic sources**, to the evidentiary standard Track A applies to EU law.
- A named counterparty, a named accountable party for safety, and criteria **set with them** — not drafted on their behalf.
- ADRs that depend on EU law re-scoped or re-decided for the Malaysian context, explicitly, in writing.

## Named accountabilities

- **The assessment's honesty, evidence discipline and published claims:** **Vpnet — Accountable.** This is the one thing that is genuinely ours to be accountable for, and it is the whole basis of the work's value.
- **Safety case & actuation authority:** the operator and its safety authority, **in any real deployment**. **Vpnet is never Accountable for safety** — unchanged, and now unambiguous, because no such deployment is in progress under Track A.
- **Track B accountabilities:** assigned when a counterparty is engaged. Until then, unassigned rather than assumed.
- **ARB:** a **working session chaired by the Vpnet engagement lead**, not a convened board of the named deciders — as recorded in `07-arb-minute-2026-08-15.md`. `Accepted (ARB)` means the decision is settled *internally*. It is not an external position.

## What this charter is not

- **Not a mandate.** No party has asked for this work or is bound by it.
- **Not a safety case, and not an input to one**, unless and until an accountable operator adopts it as such.
- **Not a regulatory submission, and not seeking to become one.** Findings may be shared with the EBA, the BSI/ACS or any other body **as contributions from an independent party**, and any such approach must say so in its opening lines. **What is never sent is a request for concurrence, endorsement or approval** — a letter that lets a regulator infer a mandate that does not exist is the single most damaging thing this engagement could send.
- **Not evidence about Malaysia.** The register is 355 rows of European evidence. Track B starts at zero.

## Guardrail restated

This is **autonomous oversight, not autonomous control.** The autonomy is in the watching and the proposing; command of safety-critical action remains with people. That boundary is what makes the system certifiable and is non-negotiable — **in either jurisdiction**.
