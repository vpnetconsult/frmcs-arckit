## Resolution 1 — adopt the `[D]` / `[I]` action-item convention

**Carried.**

An ADR records that a decision was made; it is not a completion certificate. Action items are hereafter marked:

- **`[D]` decision-shaped** — resolves what the decision *is*. Blocks ratification.
- **`[I]` implementation** — follows from the decision. Does **not** block ratification; tracked to programme gates.

**Rationale.** At 338 evidence rows the register held **0 of 9 ratified ADRs** and 55 open action items. The cause was structural: seven of nine ADRs bundled implementation into the ratification path, so no decision could be accepted until the whole programme was designed. ADR-001 was the clearest case — item 1 was the ratification itself, items 2–8 were network planning, service catalogues, field trials and a programme plan.

**Applied** to ADR-001, -002 and -003 in this session. **Action ARB-2026-08-15/01:** apply to the remaining six ADRs. Owner: engagement lead. Due: next review.

## Resolution 2 — ADR-003 (EU AI Act classification): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-15) — NSA concurrence outstanding`**

**Decision ratified:** the agentic oversight layer is **not high-risk under the EU AI Act as designed**, conditional on the Article 3(14) safety-component test.

**Basis.** The strongest evidentiary case of the nine, and the only ADR that had closed any action items (4 of 6). Verified against **primary law** — Reg (EU) 2024/1689 Art 6(1) two-part test; Annex I §B item 17 via Rail Dir (EU) 2016/797; Annex III(2) covers road traffic, **not** rail, so no independent trigger. Evidence **E-2026-06-24-07 (tier A)**.

**Condition attached (binding).** ADR-003 item 5 carries forward as a **standing design constraint, not a task to be closed**: the layer must remain non-actuating and advisory. **Any design change that moves it toward actuation re-triggers the Art 6(1)(a) test and reopens this ADR.** The constraint is policed by ADR-004.

**Noted.** This resolution is exactly the kind that needs NSA concurrence — it is a regulatory posture, and it is the basis on which a high-risk conformity programme is *not* being run.

## Resolution 3 — ADR-001 (GSM-R → FRMCS transition): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-15) — NSA concurrence outstanding`**

**Decision ratified:** Option B — dedicated rail 5G SA on RMR spectrum with an MCX service layer and gateway decoupling — as the **spine**, with Option C (public-5G / MOCN, satellite) as a **sanctioned fallback layer, not the foundation**, executed as a phased dual-network parallel run. Option D (big-bang cutover) rejected.

**Basis.** The most heavily evidenced decision in the register. Items 2–8 are implementation and do not block.

**Correction accepted into the record.** The ADR's "~2030 switch-off" premise was **withdrawn 2026-08-15**. Ratified position: **coexistence to at least 2035, with 31 Dec 2040 as the EU legal outer bound** (Reg (EU) 2026/693 Art 8(2), E-2026-08-01-11/-16); ERA window 2035–2040; DE national switch-off 2035; operator "mid-2030s"; vendor 2035/2040 commitments discounted as vendor-interested.

**The board notes the correction strengthens rather than weakens the decision, and reverses one pressure.** A *longer* parallel run makes **coexistence (R2) more load-bearing, not less** — more years of two estates, two skill sets, two assurance chains, and longer funding exposure. **Action ARB-2026-08-15/02:** carry the longer-horizon consequence into the migration programme plan (ADR-001 item 8). Owner: ERTMS Programme.

**The board further notes** the rejection of Option A must now rest on the **capability ceiling**, not on an obsolescence date — a longer GSM-R horizon makes life-extension superficially more attractive, and the original rejection leaned on a date that no longer holds.

## Resolution 4 — ADR-002 (agentic decision & oversight layer): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-15) — NSA concurrence outstanding`**

**Decision ratified:** an agentic decision and oversight layer over the FRMCS bearer, governed by decision class with a human-in-the-loop gate, in which **agents advise and never actuate**. The guardrail — **oversight, not control; human-in-command for safety-critical actuation** — is ratified as binding architecture, not as aspiration.

**Basis.** Its sole decision-shaped blocker cleared on 2026-08-15: the ADR had asserted an EU AI Act **high-risk** compliance posture as settled, which ADR-003 had disproved **the same day ADR-002 was written**. Corrected. Remaining items are build work.

**Condition attached.** ADR-002 item 3 (define the SIL-4 boundary) is decision-shaped but is **held by ADR-004, which is not ratified**. It is recorded as a **dependency, not a precondition** — ADR-002's governance decision stands; the boundary's technical definition is ADR-004's to deliver. **ADR-002 does not become operative in detailed design until ADR-004 is ratified.**

**The board notes** the corrected position removes an unnecessary cost: Art 14 oversight, logging, transparency and risk-management obligations do **not** attach automatically. Adopting them regardless would have been a self-inflicted conformity programme.

## Resolution 5 — ADR-009 (fleet retrofit): **split; pattern ratified, ADR remains Proposed**

**ADR-009 Status: unchanged — `Proposed`.**

The board **separates** two things previously bundled:

1. **The retrofit pattern** — the multi-mode two-stage retrofit as the on-board baseline (ADR-009 item 2) — is **decision-shaped and is ratified** as the working baseline, subject to confirmation with rolling-stock owners.
2. **The external dependencies** — Bund *Förderrichtlinie*, sector coordinating body, EBA *Serienzulassung* reform, chipset / Release-19 supply — remain open and are **outside the programme's control**. They continue to gate ADR-009 as a whole.

**Rationale.** An architecture decision should not be held hostage to a funding decision taken by another party. **Action ARB-2026-08-15/03:** raise the pattern as its own ADR (ADR-013) at the next session so it can carry a status independent of the funding track. Owner: engagement lead.
