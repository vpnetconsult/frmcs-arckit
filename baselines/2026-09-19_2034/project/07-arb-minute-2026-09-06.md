## Resolution 1 — Q-11: the acceptance detection route

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Do nothing** — acceptance belongs to the deployment programme, not the evaluation strategy | **Rejected.** ADR-010 exists *because* eval criteria were "scattered rather than coherent". ADR-002 naming a failure mode that no ADR measures is the exact scattering this ADR was created to end. And the division-of-labour objection fails on its own terms: item 2 already measures **humans**, not only agents |
| **1** | Add acceptance as a **fifth dimension** | **Rejected.** A fifth dimension implies a fifth promotion gate. The register should not invent a gate to house an obligation |
| **2** | Adopt **EN ISO 14915** and stop | **Rejected** — it is a **design** standard. It says how to build the interface; it cannot tell you whether users read the layer as surveillance or instruction |
| **3** | **Acceptance enters item 2 as a measured property, behavioural per /5 R3, with EN ISO 14915 adopted for the design half only and the instrument expressly unspecified** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 3).** ⚠️ **The board decides the OBLIGATION and declines to invent the INSTRUMENT.** This follows ADR-009's principle, applied at /5: *external dependencies gate the outcome, not the decision.* What is decided: acceptance is measured; it is measured behaviourally; a survey alone is not an available answer; and **EN ISO 14915 discharges the design half and nothing else**. What is **not** decided and is recorded as unspecified: what the behavioural signal for "reads as surveillance" or "reads as instruction" actually is. ⛔ **The board expressly refuses to nominate dissent rate for this: /5 R1 established that dissent carries two readings, and pressing an ambiguous signal into a second job would compound the error rather than solve it.** **Owner: engagement lead. Review: ADR-010's next quarterly.**

## Resolution 3 — Q-15 taken BEFORE Q-13, because Q-13's third limb depends on it

**Is DIN VDE V 0831-104 still required at all?**

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Leave 9b exactly as written — the instruments are incommensurable | **Rejected.** The incommensurability is partial, not total: the matrix's **Exposure** and **Vulnerability** columns are doing the threat-side work −104's scales were wanted for |
| **1** | Adopt SP/ERORAT as the register's quantitative route; reduce 9b to a note that −104 was never needed | ⚠️ **Rejected as over-reach.** The register has read ERORAT's *process*; it has **not** read ERORAT's scale definitions against the use −104 was wanted for. Declaring the need extinguished on an unread comparison would repeat the error this register keeps making |
| **2** | **Adopt the SP/ERORAT scheme as the register's quantitative route, and NARROW 9b's residual to what actually remains unverified** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 2).** **The residual is not lifted and not left as written — it is narrowed, and the change is one of kind: from *"no normative quantitative scale is held"* (false as of 2026-09-06) to *"ERORAT's scale definitions have not been read against the use −104 was wanted for"* (true, and closable by reading a document the register already holds).** ⚠️ **That distinction matters more than the outcome: the first residual invited a purchase; the second invites a read. The register accepted a permanent residual for sixteen days that a free document in its own possession may already discharge — and the reason it stood is that nobody re-asked the question once it was marked *permanent*. "Permanent" is a word this register should use about a residual only where it means "not answerable by any obtainable evidence", and it did not mean that here.** **Action item added to ADR-012: read ERORAT's scale definitions against 9b's stated need.**

## Resolution 4 — Q-13: the supplier clause set, and CVSS

**(a) and (b) CARRIED.** Decision item 1's procurement gate adopts, as named and verifiable bid conditions: supplier **ISO 27001** for the development organisation and **IEC 62443-4-1 minimum ML 3**; service provider **ISO 27001 + IEC 62443-2-4 minimum ML 3** (ML 3 = CMMI Level 3); lifecycle personnel background checks; a single security point of contact; **tamper-evident transport seals specified against a documented risk communicated before delivery**, with transport-step tracking. Same ceiling as R2.

⚠️ **A CONSEQUENCE THE BOARD RECORDS RATHER THAN DISCOVERS LATER: this gate hard-codes a size filter.** E-2026-08-19-13 measured security maturity correlating with organisation size (r = .42), and ADR-013 constraint 3 already requires *"a supported variant for operators without an in-house security function."* **Requiring ISO 27001 plus CMMI ML 3 certification will exclude small suppliers and small EVUs from conforming supply. That may be the right answer for safety-relevant procurement, but it is a distributional choice being made by a technical clause, and it is minuted as such.**

**(c) CVSS 4.0 — NOT connected to 9b.** CVSS scores the severity of a **known vulnerability**; ERORAT's scheme scores **risk**; −104 supplies **attacker potential**. Three axes, not one. **CVSS 4.0 Base + Threat is adopted where it belongs — item 4's reporting clause — and 9b is untouched by it.** ⛔ The board notes this is the third time an over-connection was proposed and the second time it was caught by testing rather than by intuition.

## Resolution 6 — Q-16: ADR-013's ratification condition. ⚠️ The consequential one.

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Do not discharge — anonymised aggregate is not confirmation with owners | **Rejected.** 26 RUs across 20 countries, collected by EU-Rail's own deployment management team and reviewed before publication, is the best confirmation this condition will ever get. Holding out for a named-owner sign-off the engagement has no standing to obtain would leave the condition open forever, which is not rigour |
| **1** | Discharge | **Rejected** — see below |
| **2** | Discharge partially, for owner-operators only | **Carried in part** |
| **3** | **Amend the scope FIRST, then discharge for the pattern, with the lessor gap recorded** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 3).** ⚠️⚠️ **THE REASON THE BOARD WOULD NOT SIMPLY DISCHARGE: the same evidence that confirms the pattern shows the SCOPE IS WRONG.** *"Except for 2 RUs all consider a **combined FRMCS and ETCS modification** for their fleet."* ADR-013's two stages, its approval-trigger analysis at constraint 6 (EIGV / Reg (EU) 2018/545) and its downtime arithmetic are all computed for **FRMCS alone**. **Discharging a ratification condition on evidence that simultaneously shows the ADR governs the wrong intervention would be closing the wrong loop with the right document.**

**Therefore: (i) the condition in ADR-013's Status line is DISCHARGED as to THE PATTERN — mechanism and reason both confirmed; (ii) a new action item is opened for the SCOPE question — are the stages specified for an FRMCS retrofit or for the combined intervention the sector actually plans; (iii) the LESSOR GAP is recorded as an express bound: respondents are undertakings, and the report separately states that leasing RUs and lessors will require third-party support, so leased fleet is not covered by this discharge.** ⚠️ **And the scope pressure is two-sided: E-2026-09-06-04 records a second, non-FRMCS on-board radio (UWB train-to-train SRC) that may want the same vehicle touch. ADR-013's economics rest on minimising approval-triggering interventions per vehicle — an argument that weakens each time another programme claims the window.**

THE REGISTER CONVERTED INVISIBLE DRIFT INTO VISIBLE ARREARS AND CALLED IT A CONTROL.
