## Context

ADR-001's parallel run needs on the order of **16,000–21,000 DE vehicles** FRMCS-capable by ~2035. The per-vehicle intervention pattern is the one lever the architecture actually controls (ADR-009 §Trade-off). The DKS pilot established the working precedent: a **multi-mode, two-stage retrofit** at a few €k per EMU within an "aus einem Guss" approach, federally funded for 333 regional EMUs, alongside FRMCS-ready new builds (130 Alstom Coradia Max + 28 Siemens Mireo) (E-2026-06-24-21).

## Decision

Adopt the **multi-mode, two-stage retrofit** as the on-board baseline:

- **Stage 1 — during any scheduled vehicle touch:** antennas, cabling, netboxes — the invasive, approval-light physical preparation, batched into maintenance the vehicle undergoes anyway. **Restated ARB-2026-09-19 R1 (Q-19): stage 1 is intervention-agnostic** — TOBA-7540 §8.3.4's per-vehicle "target configuration" and advance preparation of "equipment positions, racks/space, cabling, antennas and their positions" serve the cab-radio and the ETCS migration alike (E-2026-09-19-21).
- **Stage 2 — near FRMCS service availability:** the application-specific fit, deferred until chipset/Release maturity, minimising the window between spend and use. **Split ARB-2026-09-19 R1 (Q-19) into two objects, because the sector plans a combined FRMCS + ETCS intervention (E-2026-09-06-03: all but 2 of 26 RUs) and TOBA-7540 foresees exactly that with "no fundamental difference from an architecture point of view" whichever migrates first (§8.3.3):**
  - **Stage 2a — cab radio (voice):** modems/filters + software update; TOBA-7540 Cab Radio Variant 4a (existing GSM-R cab radio gains an FRMCS voice function over OBAPP, GSM-R path unchanged) or 4b (new dual cab radio). The authorisation object constraint 6 was computed for.
  - **Stage 2b — ETCS data path:** TOBA-7540 ETCS Variant 3 — Euroradio modified to the OBAPP interface, CS/PS bypass to the GSM-R EDOR (recommended for new OBUs and for existing ones where feasible; Variant 1b protocol conversion only where an OBU upgrade is infeasible). **A different authorisation object** — a CCS on-board modification (VDV 2021: *"Einzelfallbetrachtung erforderlich für Anwendungsfälle ETCS und Datenübertragung"*, E-2026-09-15-02) — assessed separately from 2a even when fitted in the same touch.
  - **Invariant across both:** ETCS and voice radios stay strictly separated (TOBA-7540 §8.1.1); redundant modules are added within a role, never shared.
- **Multi-mode throughout:** hardware capable of GSM-R and FRMCS operation across the coexistence window, so a vehicle is never stranded on either side of the cutover.
- **New builds FRMCS-ready by procurement clause** (ADR-009 item 3), so the retrofit population shrinks through fleet renewal.

The pattern minimises per-vehicle cost and — decisively for R13, where the approval burden is ~30% of programme cost — **the number of approval-triggering interventions per vehicle**.

## Constraints attached by later evidence

1. **Variant-spanning test coverage (E-2026-08-15-54).** NS's fleet retrofit lost three weeks of series production to a fleet-configuration variant (4-coach IP plan) not represented in testing. Stage-1/stage-2 test benches must enumerate fleet/configuration variants (car count, looms, switch/AP topology, coach-level addressing), not just the modal configuration. Carried as ADR-009 item 5 and into the ADR-007 pre-change gate.
2. **Execution-rate grounding (E-2026-08-15-54).** The NS programme (~1200 units, first fleet in 4 months, 1 train/day, 4 mechanics/day, pre-try-out + 4 try-outs, daily second-line support) is the order-of-magnitude planning basis — NL fleet, cyber appliance not radio swap; not a transferable rate. Prefer field-replaceable units with auto-detect-and-install.
3. **Capability spread (E-2026-08-19-13).** The retrofit population includes NE-Bahnen and small EVU measurably least able to secure what they fit (maturity correlates with size, r = .42). The pattern needs a supported variant for operators without an in-house security function — carried as ADR-009 item 7.
4. **RTO fitment may share the same window (E-2026-08-20-05).** RemODtrAIn builds its remote-operation prototype explicitly for retrofit as well as new vehicles; the same estate may face FRMCS and RTO fitment against the same approval bottleneck. Watch for stage-2 scope growth.
5. **The pattern corroborated in the wild, on the DKS fleet itself (E-2026-08-20-26).** The AutomatedTrain BR 430 — already ETCS-fitted under Digitaler Knoten Stuttgart — took its sensing retrofit as a second integration on that prepared base, with **ATO-OB following in a named second Ausrüstungsstufe**: stage-1/stage-2 in practice. Integration constraints to carry into stage planning: **UIC 651 driver sight-field** limits on windscreen equipment; **antenna minimum separations against existing ETCS antennas** (eight new antennas needed layout care); **reversibility as a contractual premise** (existing fixing points reused, drillings restored on removal); and reuse of type-approved sensors via signal doublers cleared by **SVoC** rather than new fitment — an approval-burden saving in exactly the currency R13 is short of. **A fifth constraint from the radio spec itself (E-2026-09-19-16, TS 38.101-1 V20.0.0): the FRMCS cab radio is a 3GPP-defined UE class — power class 1 (31 dBm) in n100/n101 "allowed only for FRMCS cab-radio's, i.e. user equipments installed on trains with external antenna on top of the train roof at approximately 4 m over ground", dual-band CA/DC n100+n101 at PC1 (Rel-19), and Annex M's mandatory manufacturer declaration of the post-connector antenna gain G_n100/n101, with conducted limits scaled where the resulting PEIRP exceeds 33 dBm. Stage 1's antenna position, feeder loss and antenna gain therefore enter stage 2's conformance declaration: the declared gain is a per-vehicle-type parameter of the retrofit specification, and a later change of antenna or feeder is a change to that declaration (ADR-011 class), not a maintenance act. The sector's RAN report says the same from the network side (E-2026-09-19-21, UGFA O-8868 §4.1–4.2): all on-board radios PC1; UE features by release (Rel-17 n100/n101/HST, Rel-18 PC1 / <5 MHz n100 / CHO, Rel-19 CA-DC and 2×2 UL MIMO requested); cable loss for integrated vs distributed TOBA, remote radio module + antenna, on-board coexistence and additional RF filtering — stage-1 layout inputs. And a sixth: **ETCS and voice radios stay strictly separated** (TOBA-7540 §8.1.1) — redundant modules are added within a role, never shared across the two.**
6. **The stage split matches the authorisation law (E-2026-08-20-28).** **Scope note ARB-2026-09-19 R1 (Q-19): this analysis was computed for the cab-radio path and stands for stage 2a. Stage 2b (Euroradio → OBAPP) is a CCS on-board modification and is assessed as its own object — the Table 7.1 ETCS rows and the VDV 2021 precedent apply, not the *Notruffunktion* trigger below; the "once per vehicle type via Art 15 type-versioning" economy applies to each object separately, and a combined touch does not merge them.** Read against the current EIGV and Reg (EU) 2018/545: **stage 1 is engineerable below both authorisation triggers** (EU categories (a)/(b); the national Anlage-4 § 4.2.3 below-threshold path — interfaces unchanged, in-subsystem, certified), while **stage 2 concentrates the trigger** — migrating the railway emergency call to MCX touches the nationally listed *Notruffunktion beim Zugfunk* and raises the Art 21(12)(b) safety-level question — and is exercised **once per vehicle type** via Art 15 type-versioning. The pattern's economics are therefore not just cost engineering; they are the shape the authorisation law rewards. **⚠️ SHARPENED 2026-09-15 (E-2026-09-15-02) — the statute-derived reading now has a worked precedent, and the trigger is more mechanical than "raises the safety-level question".** The 2021 GSM-R *Störfestigkeit* device swap (VDV deck for the joint ERA/EBA workshop of 25.11.2021) stayed in **category (b)** — NoBo re-assessment of the affected Table 4.1 rows, no new authorisation — *only* because CCS TSI § 7.2.1a.3 condition (1) held: **target functionality unchanged**, settled by EBA Fachmitteilung 11/2021. **Table 7.1 row 4.2.4 ("GSM-R baseline") asks exactly whether the new device means "a different baseline"; a different baseline that fails § 7.2.1a.3 is column 5 → category (d) → new authorisation.** An FRMCS radio is a different baseline by definition and cannot take the 2021 route, so **stage 2 triggers on the TSI-parameter branch (Art 21(12)(a)/(c)), not only on the (b) safety-level branch.** ⚠️ *Residual: the 2021 deck cites the 2019-numbered TSI; 7. **Stage 2b is under-specified for the fleet it is written for (E-2026-09-20-02 — CTO-Council working group, solution stream, *"FRMCS – ETCS Baseline 3 (non-)compatibility / Technical Report"*, April 2024, v1.0, DB · NS · SBB · Siemens · Thales · Kontron · Funkwerk · EUG; Ares(2024)7304542).** Stage 2b names TOBA-7540 ETCS Variant 3 — Euroradio modified to OBAPP — with Variant 1b only where an OBU upgrade is infeasible. The report says what "modifying Euroradio" means for the existing fleet: **under TSI 23, ETCS over FRMCS requires on-board system version 3.0 (Baseline 4).** For the Baseline 3 vehicles at SV 2.0 / 2.1 that make up the retrofit population, that leaves **three routes, not one, and they are three different authorisation objects:** **(i) a BL4 SV 3.0 upgrade**, which applies CR1359 and so changes SS-026 — the SIL-4 core (ADR-004's kernel) is re-assessed, and the vehicle gains SV 2.2/3.0 functions it may not need; **(ii) "Baseline Light"** — the B4R1 Euroradio subsets combined with the vehicle's existing set of specifications 2 or 3, two alterations to SS-037-1 v4.0.0 (bearer selection without balise packet 245; a faked GSM-R registration towards the ETCS application after GSM-R switch-off), no SS-026 change, no DMI change, "does not affect the SIL4 core of ETCS" — submitted to ERA as a joint EUG–UNISIG concept on 28.03.2024, **change requests unwritten and the certification regime "must be agreed with ERA"**; it is the BL3 realisation of Variant 3 and is explicitly an intermediate step to SV 3.y; **(iii) an adapter** — Solution A for SV 2.0 (= TOBA Variant 1b: X.225/T.70/HDLC↔IP conversion, "no risk mitigation measures could be found"), Solution D for SV 2.1 (DNS extended proxy, adapter transmission-mode table, faked GPRS attach/PDP after switch-off; the preferred adapter) — with Euroradio timing/race and supplier-stack-variation risks open since the 2019 Unitel assessment, each build "most likely specific to each relevant vendor combination", and **"it is not clear whether the necessary supplier ecosystem will be available"**. Solutions B, C, E1, E2, F were eliminated (ISDN kept alive; vanilla 5G without FRMCS QoS/TLS; trackside de-tunnellers/multipath gateways). **Trackside coupling, which the register did not carry:** as long as any Baseline-Light or adapter vehicle uses a track, its RBC stays at **SV 2.3 "FRMCS-only mode"** — no SV 3.y RBC, hence no DAC or improved ATO on that track — until every such vehicle is retired or upgraded; balise packet 45 (GSM-R registration order) must remain after switch-off; GSM-R ISDN interfaces may go. The route chosen for the fleet is therefore also the trackside roadmap and an interoperability question (the report asks whether "all IMs" must carry a trackside impact "if a single EVU somewhere in Europe" certifies such vehicles). **Observability note for ADR-002 item 9:** the report states that trackside radio infrastructure and ERTMS monitoring "cannot / should not detect the usage of the adapter onboard" — adapter vehicles are invisible to trackside diagnosis by design, and the RU must supply the tooling. **Bounds:** April 2024 proposals, not decisions; ERA's disposition and the fate of CR1359 in Reg 2026/693 are not held (`14-next-acts.md` B20); the report's phrase "IMs notifying end of GSM-R service from 2030 onwards with a five-year notice period" is the working group's planning reading — the register's verified §7.3.1.2 text (E-2026-09-19-29) has one 5-year clock gated on the V3 amendment and no 2030 floor was quoted; re-check the consolidated text on the host before either reading is used. **Carried as PR17e and as item 4.**

8. **The gateway-fronted cab radio has an unsolved NAT problem in multi-vendor test (E-2026-09-26-05 — 3rd ETSI FRMCS Plugtests Report, Jul 2023, §10.1.7, tier A).** The on-board arrangement this pattern relies on — cab radios (MCX clients) behind one or more MC gateway UEs that also serve the rest of the train — was found in test to break SIP and RTP reachability: the cab radio's private address travels in the SIP layer while the gateway's address is in the IP layer, so the server's replies and media cannot reach the cab radio, and per-device port mapping on the gateway breaks RTP/RTCP. Bridge mode and DHCP address-mirroring were ruled out; STUN (no server deployed), a SIP proxy or SIP-ALG in the gateway (not all gateways have one) and RFC 6263 keep-alives (implemented differently, non-standard) were each found wanting; the report asks for "a clarification regarding the usage (or forbidden usage) of NAT in such scenarios". **Consequence for stage 2a/2b:** the OB-GW procurement and the cab-radio variant 4a/4b interface must state which of the three mitigations the fleet standardises on, or require gateway-resident SIP-ALG — the FIS/FFFIS do not decide it, and the 2024 and 2025 reports carry no closure of this observation. Open; no action item yet — raise at the next ADR-001 item 4 on-board architecture touch.

## Consequences

- **Easier:** ADR-001 item 4 (TOBA/on-board architecture) has a settled fitment pattern to design against; retrofit economics survive the funding track's uncertainty; the decision no longer shares a status with dependencies it doesn't control.
- **Harder:** two touches per vehicle need two scheduling windows; stage-2 timing is chipset/Release-gated (ADR-009 dependency track); the multi-mode window carries dual-equipment cost for the coexistence duration.
- **To revisit:** if the *Serienzulassung*/component-authorisation reform (ADR-009 item 9 decision) changes the approval arithmetic, the stage boundaries may be redrawn; revisit on ARTE's regulatory-obstacles deliverable.
