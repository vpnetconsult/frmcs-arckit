## Resolution 1 — ADR-003: amendment under Reg (EU) 2026/1744 **ratified as an amendment to a ratified decision**

**Carried.** ADR-003 (Accepted, ARB-2026-08-15 R2) rests on the Art 6 / Art 3(14) safety-component test. **Reg (EU) 2026/1744 (in force 27.7.2026) replaced the operative definitional ground** — Art 6(1a)–(1c) now state in statute when a system is and is not a safety component. That is primary law read at source: exactly the standard for reopening a ratified decision, and it goes through the board as an amendment, not folded in.

**The board ratifies the amendment as recorded in the ADR (2026-08-23, E-2026-08-23-02):**

1. The **conclusion stands** — not high-risk as designed — now resting on Art 6(1a)'s explicit carve-out rather than on interpretation of Art 3(14) alone.
2. The **standing constraint's re-run trigger is amended**: under Art 6(1b), any change after which the layer's **failure or malfunctioning could endanger health and safety** re-runs the test — not only moves toward actuation. A misleading advisory inducing dangerous human action is inside that question; ADR-004's FFI evidence and ADR-010's automation-bias metrics are what bound it.
3. Art 6(1c) is noted for ADR-012: spectrum/EMC-only third-party assessment creates no Art 6(1)(b) exposure.
4. **Sequencing acknowledged for the record:** the amendment text was written into the ADR on 2026-08-23, the same day the law was read, ahead of board authorisation. The follow-through standard is amendment-to-the-board first; this minute regularises it and the board adopts the text as written, unchanged. The gap between edit and authorisation was two agenda turns, and is recorded rather than smoothed over.
