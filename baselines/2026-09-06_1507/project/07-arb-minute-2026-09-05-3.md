## Resolution 1 — ⚠️ the chair's stated reason for NOT convening was wrong, and is withdrawn

The chair twice declined to convene on Q-6, on the ground that *"its revised proposal depends on item-1 accuracy evidence, which requires an estate that does not exist."*

**That conflated two different things.** Q-6's revised proposal is a rule about **how a metric is reported** — a decision about eval design, which is what ADR-010 is for. **It does not require the data to exist. One can decide today that a number must never be published alone, with no instance of that number yet in hand.**

**The register already holds the distinction the chair failed to apply**: ADR-009's ratification records that *"the external dependencies gate the OUTCOME, not the DECISION."* **Q-6 was decidable from the day it was raised; the delay was the chair's error, not a property of the item.** Recorded because the same error — treating an unmeasurable thing as an undecidable one — would silently park any number of eval decisions behind an estate that may never exist.

## Resolution 2 — dissent-rate reporting

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Do nothing** — item 2 stands; dissent remains reportable alone, with the band reading as the only guard | **Rejected.** The band flags *both ends* but cannot say *which* of three incompatible explanations produced the number |
| **1** | Require the tension to be *named* in each report, leaving the reader to weigh it | **Rejected** — a caveat is not a control; it survives exactly as long as whoever writes the report remembers it |
| **2** | **Dissent may never be reported alone: always with accuracy and calibration, in the same view** | **✅ CARRIED** |
| **3** | Retire dissent rate entirely as uninterpretable | **Rejected** — it remains a genuine signal; the defect is isolation, not the measure |

**Carried (Option 2).** Every report of item 2(a) carries item 1's **accuracy evidence** for that agent and item 2(d)'s **calibration** cell. **Dissent is a behavioural fact with at least three incompatible explanations — the human is rubber-stamping; the human is exercising judgement; or the advice is not worth following — and dissent alone cannot distinguish them. Calibration is what separates "followed because the advice was good" from "followed because following is easier."** The band reading **stands but is subordinated: a signal to investigate, never a verdict.**

## Resolution 3 — compliance rate at the promotion gate

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Do nothing** — no rule; rely on the assessor's judgement not to misuse a following rate | **Rejected.** The misuse is attractive precisely because a high following rate *looks* like success |
| **1** | Permit it as *supporting* evidence alongside calibration | **Rejected** — it would still be evidence about behaviour dressed as evidence about merit |
| **2** | **Prohibit a compliance/following rate as evidence of oversight quality; record operational compliance cases as business arguments** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 2).** ⚠️ **A high following rate is evidence about the HUMAN'S BEHAVIOUR, not the SYSTEM'S MERIT; treating it as quality evidence inverts the measure and rewards exactly the rubber-stamping item 2 exists to detect.** FAS-D's and FASaN's operational cases for higher compliance are recorded as **business** arguments, never oversight-effectiveness ones.

## Resolution 4 — ⚠️ the failure mode neither metric detects

Both FAS-D/FASaN (low compliance = risk to engineer away) and ADR-010 item 2 (low dissent = safety warning) **presuppose the advice is worth following.** Six drivers interviewed by DLR estimated rail ADAS's **usefulness as LOW** and preferred information they could act on over a system that — their word — **"patronizes"** them.

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Do nothing** — leave it in the evidence log; six interviews is too thin to move an ADR | **Rejected, but it was the closest call in this session.** The finding does not depend on the driver evidence being representative: **the failure mode is structural and would exist even if every driver loved the system** |
| **1** | Add a metric for "advice value" | **Rejected** — it would be invented, and item 1 + item 5(d) already provide the honest route |
| **2** | **Record a standing question, not a metric, and gate promotion on it** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 2), with the finding stated:**

> **A useless advisory system produces excellent-looking oversight numbers.** If the advice adds little, competent humans correctly ignore it; dissent is high, calibration may look sound, and every item-2 measure reads as healthy oversight — while the layer delivers nothing. **The framework can confirm that humans are exercising judgement without ever asking whether the system deserved their attention.**

**Standing question — "is the advice worth following?" — answerable only by item 1's accuracy work against the item 5(d) reference system. No promotion while it is unanswered, however good the oversight measures look.** ⚠️ **Bound: six interviews in a conference abstract — a signal that the question is open, NOT a finding about what train drivers think.**

## Resolution 5 — the information / instruction distinction

The same source distinguishes **information the driver can act on** from **instruction they are expected to follow**, and rejects the second. **ADR-002's guardrail protects against actuation and says nothing about whether the advisory layer patronises the people it advises.**

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Do nothing** — leave it in the evidence log until a second source exists | **Genuinely arguable and expressly preserved — see below** |
| **1** | Amend ADR-002 now | **Rejected.** ADR-002 is `Accepted`, and R1c makes the queue the only write path; amending a ratified decision on six interviews would be exactly the over-reach this register guards against |
| **2** | **Queue it as Q-8, with Option 0 preserved as a live disposition for the deciding session** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 2).** **Q-8 is opened and carries its own Option 0 explicitly: the deciding board may properly conclude that a signal this thin does not justify amending a ratified decision, and hold until a second source exists.** Queueing is not a commitment to amend.
