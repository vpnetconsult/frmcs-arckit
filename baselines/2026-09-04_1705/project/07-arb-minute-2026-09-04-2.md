## Resolution 1 — Q-1: ADR-010 amendment **carried, with one substantive change to the proposal as queued**

**ADR-010 is `Accepted (ARB-2026-08-20/2)`.** Q-1 proposed three additions arising from E-2026-09-04-09 (Müller, arXiv:2504.02664). The board takes them one at a time rather than as a block, because they do not rest on equally firm ground.

### 1a — Item 12: criterion drift. **Carried AS A HYPOTHESIS TO BE MEASURED, not as an established property.**

**The proposal as queued** would have item 12 record that human adjudicators score an AI on **risk relevance** rather than **task correctness**, leaving the label stream systematically mis-specified.

⚠️ **The board declines to carry that as a stated property, for a reason the proposal did not confront.** The study's participants were **laboratory participants rating a simulated AI on image sequences**. Item 12's adjudicators are **qualified rail personnel doing an operational job they are accountable for** — in the *Schwelle 3.0* precedent the item is built on, *Streckenverantwortliche* adjudicating findings on their own line. **Whether criterion drift survives that transfer is exactly what the study does not test.** Recording it as a property of our adjudicators would be the same defect this board struck in the first session today: a careful reading inflated into a finding.

**What is carried instead, and why it is not a weaker outcome:**

1. Item 12 records criterion drift as a **named, testable hazard distinct from the anchoring hazard already there** — anchoring is *the reviewer influenced by the model's output*; criterion drift is *the reviewer scoring the wrong question*. Two different failures, two different tests.
2. **A measurement obligation attaches:** the eval harness must be able to detect whether adjudicator scores correlate with **risk-relevant context the model cannot perceive**. That is cheap to test — the context variables are known, and the correlation either appears or it does not.
3. **The asymmetry justifies acting before the evidence is in.** If the hypothesis is false, the measurement costs little. If it is true and unmeasured, every downstream eval rests on labels mis-specified against the trained task. **The board acts on the cost asymmetry, and says so, rather than pretending the generalisation is established.**

### 1b — Item 1: fix and document the elicitation method. **Carried as proposed.**

The study found **the elicitation method itself moved the ratings.** This needs no generalisation argument to bite: if how you ask changes the answer, then the elicitation procedure is part of the measuring instrument. **Item 1 gains the requirement to fix and document it, and to treat a change of elicitation as a change to the instrument — re-baselining the metric, not continuing the series.**

### 1c — Item 2: the miss/false-alarm asymmetry. **Carried as proposed, narrowed to a prohibition.**

The board carries this as a **prohibition rather than a finding**, because the register holds no rail measurement of it: **do not inherit the miss/false-alarm cost ordering from automotive, medical or aviation work.** The study reports the ordering is contingent on task difficulty; in rail, false alarms cause unnecessary braking and misses risk fatalities, and **the register has no measurement of the trade in its own domain.** Until it does, the ordering is an open parameter of the eval design, not a default.

### Status

**ADR-010 remains `Accepted`.** This is an amendment to a ratified decision, minuted as such, and **written into the ADR only now that it is authorised** — the sequence ARB-2026-09-04 R4 required, applied for the first time.
