## Resolution 1 — Q-19: which intervention the pattern governs

**Options put:**

- **Option 0 — leave open.** Item stays `[D]`-open with the evidence marked ready.
- **Option 1 — combined scope only.** Restate the pattern as governing the combined FRMCS + ETCS intervention: stage 1 unchanged and intervention-agnostic; stage 2 split into **2a** cab radio (Variant 4a/4b) and **2b** ETCS Variant 3 (Euroradio modified to OBAPP, CS/PS bypass to the GSM-R EDOR) with its own authorisation object; constraint 6 recomputed for the combined scope. Close item 2.
- **Option 2 — FRMCS-alone confirmed.** Keep the pattern scoped to FRMCS; record combined interventions as a programme bound outside this ADR; close item 2; open a separate item for the ETCS path.
- **Option 3 — Option 1 plus the ERA machine-readable rules.** As Option 1, and open one `[I]` item carrying: (i) the stage-1/stage-2a/2b split expressed as Reg 2018/545 authorisation graphs the ERA `va-m2m` test bed can validate; (ii) the on-board facts this ADR fixes (PC1 cab-radio class, declared post-connector antenna gain, ETCS/voice radio separation, per-vehicle target configuration) written in ERA Ontology (ERATV/EVR) terms from the start.

**Carried: Option 3 (of four).**

**Reasoning recorded.** The sector's own migration document foresees exactly the combined intervention the questionnaire reports, and says the architecture does not care which application migrates first — so the pattern *does* govern the combined case, and the item's fear ("stage boundaries in the wrong places") resolves into a *split of stage 2*, not a redrawing of stage 1. Stage 1's physical preparation is intervention-agnostic in TOBA-7540's words. What was wrong was narrower than the item feared: the Decision described only the cab-radio half of stage 2, and constraint 6 costed only that half. Option 2 was rejected because it would keep the ADR describing a fitment the sector is not planning. Option 1 was rejected in favour of Option 3 because the authorisation object — the thing item 2 was really about — is being made machine-assessable by the Agency now, and the cheapest way to *settle* "right intervention" going forward is to express each stage as the authorisation graph the assessor will read; deferring that to a note would leave the item's question re-openable by the next evidence row.
