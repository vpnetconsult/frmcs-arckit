## Resolution 1 — ADR-009 (fleet retrofit as managed external dependency): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-20) — settled internally; no external validation sought or available`**

**Decision ratified:** Option A — fleet-scale rolling-stock FRMCS readiness treated as an **explicit, tracked external dependency and precondition** of the ADR-001 parallel run, never as an assumption; the architecture's leverage applied where it genuinely exists — the on-board pattern (carried in **ADR-013**, Accepted per ARB-2026-08-15 R5) and FRMCS-readiness procurement clauses — with funding flow, approval throughput and chipset/Release supply tracked as named risks owned outside the programme.

**Minuted with the decision:**

1. **The item-9 working assumption is part of the ratified position:** route (a) — **generic component authorisation** — as the working assumption for fleet-scale approval arithmetic; the *Automationsinsel* route treated as complementary migration topology, not a competing fleet answer. Its recorded caveats are minuted unchanged: ARTE's authorisation covers *Probefahrten*, not revenue service; this is a planning assumption, not a prediction of what the EBA will authorise. **Review trigger:** ARTE's regulatory-obstacles deliverable and its GoA 3/4/RTO approval roadmap; tracked in the item-4 quarterly refresh.
2. **The board declines to move R13**, and notes this is required by the decision itself, not in tension with it: the requirement ("feasible & funded") is satisfied by parties outside the programme; the *posture governing it* is what this resolution accepts. R13 stays `Open — external dependency`, now under an Accepted ADR.
3. Residuals carried forward as `[I]`: items 1, 3–8 (risk-register stand-up, procurement clause, dependency refresh, variant coverage, NS-grounded sizing, capability spread, predictability reframing).

## Resolution 2 — ADR-012 (cybersecurity regulatory conformance): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-20) — settled internally; no external validation sought or available`**

**Decision ratified:** CRA + NIS-2 conformance — extended 2026-08-18/20 to the **Radio Equipment Directive** as the third applicable regime — as a **first-class governed dimension** of the transition and of the oversight layer itself, reusing the ADR-004 boundary, ADR-007 test surfaces and ADR-011 change gate rather than a parallel security process; **one gate for change**, with the adjudication rule decided in both halves: security findings enter the gate as **named hazards** in the safety side's certified vocabulary (item 9), an unmappable finding escalates the change one class, and the quantitative calibration is re-based on free primary instruments — CSM-RA severity classes and targets on the safety side, engagement-defined attacker scales on the security side (item 9b).

**Minuted with the decision:**

1. **The board explicitly owns the DIN VDE V 0831-103/-104 no-purchase consequence** (owner decision of 2026-08-20): the gate's quantitative calibration is the engagement's own and says so wherever it appears; **no conformity with, or derivation from, either document is claimed anywhere in the register**; the un-held normative scales are a **permanently accepted residual**, re-opened only if an assurance phase brings licensed access. Ratification is granted **with** this residual, not despite an expectation it will close.
2. **Two watches minuted as programme milestones:** (i) the future **Commission act repealing or amending Del Reg (EU) 2022/30** — the RED→CRA displacement event, which has **no date in the CRA** (E-2026-08-20-23d); (ii) the **ERJU System Pillar four-specification publication status** (item 8) — an interface verification, not a decision blocker.
3. **The delivery-risk finding stands beside the ratification, unsoftened:** the sector's measured Respond capability is 1.58/5 and the infrastructure managers total 1.15/5 (E-2026-08-19-13, indicative). The decision assumes conformance is a delivery programme, not a paperwork exercise.
4. Residuals carried forward as `[I]`: items 0b, 0c, 1, 2, 2b, 3, 4, 5 (inventory incl. positioning and SOVD-class diagnostic servers, procurement gate incl. the diagnostics TLS mandate, expedited-but-gated path, SMS embedding on the § 32 BSIG n.F. wording, detection extension on the § 31 Abs. 2 footing).
