## Resolution 2 — ⚠️ what the test found instead, which is a real defect and a different one

**ADR-002 item 3 makes two acceptance claims and provides no way to know whether either has failed.**

- *"An oversight layer that reads to staff as surveillance will be rejected by the safety culture it depends on, however technically sound"* (adopted 2026-08-18, E-2026-08-18-08).
- *"A layer can avoid reading as surveillance and still fail by telling a competent professional how to do their job"* (adopted ARB-2026-09-05/4 R3).

Against them item 3 sets three **design constraints** — output addressed to a named human role; stated purpose is shared learning; augment the *Fachreferent:in Betriebssicherheit* rather than substitute for it. **All three are things one can build. None is a thing one can observe having gone wrong.** There is no threshold, no signal and no owner. **The register has twice adopted a failure mode into a ratified decision and twice declined to say how it would be detected** — which is the same defect this register was founded on: a position that cannot be falsified cannot drift *visibly*, so it looks settled indefinitely.

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Do nothing** — item 3 is a specification, and specifications need not carry their own acceptance tests | ⚠️ **Rejected, though it is the strongest Option 0 the board has faced.** It is right about the division of labour and wrong about the consequence: if ADR-002 states a failure mode and ADR-010 does not measure it, **no ADR owns it**, and each is individually defensible while the gap sits between them |
| **1** | Add an acceptance-measurement obligation to ADR-002 item 3 | **Rejected.** It would put measurement in the specification ADR and break the division of labour that makes ADR-010 the single evaluation home — the scattering ADR-010 was created to end |
| **2** | **Record that the obligation belongs in ADR-010, and route it there** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Carried (Option 2).** **The two acceptance failure modes named in ADR-002 item 3 — surveillance and instruction — require a detection route in ADR-010, which currently has four dimensions (accuracy · oversight effectiveness · drift · gates) and no acceptance dimension at all.** ⚠️ **And R3 of ARB-2026-09-05/5 already governs what that route may look like before it is written: acceptance measured by asking is measuring intention, and intention did not predict compliance (r = .64 for usefulness; nil for behavioural intention). So the route cannot be a survey alone — which is the finding Q-10 was reaching for, arriving at the correct destination by a false premise.**

## Resolution 4 — the rule earned from two instances

Q-7 and Q-10 share one shape: **a queue entry asserted that an ADR contains a provision, and it did not.** Both cost a board session to discover. Both were detectable by reading the named ADR.

| | Option | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Do nothing — two instances is thin, and both were caught | **Rejected.** They were caught because the chair happened to test them; nothing required it. The precedent set at /5 R5 was a rule earned from exactly two instances |
| **1** | Require the entry's author to verify before filing | **Rejected as insufficient** — the author is the one who formed the belief, and both errors were made in good faith at filing time |
| **2** | **Require the entry to CITE the provision's location, and make verification the convened board's first act** | **✅ CARRIED** |

**Rule adopted: a queue entry asserting that an `Accepted` ADR contains a provision must cite WHERE — item number and the words relied on. The first act of the board convened on it is to open that ADR and check. Where the provision is not there, the entry is REJECTED AS FRAMED and the verification is recorded in the minute, so the rejection is checkable rather than asserted.** ⚠️ **The failure mode is specific and it is not carelessness: an amendment proposal is written from memory of what an ADR says, memory of a register this size is unreliable, and a proposal is at its most persuasive exactly when its author has just finished reasoning their way to it.**
