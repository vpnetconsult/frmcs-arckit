## Resolution 1 — ADR-010 (agent evaluation strategy): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-20/2) — settled internally; no external validation sought or available`**

**Decision ratified:** the single agent-evaluation framework of four measured dimensions — per-role accuracy, oversight-effectiveness/automation-bias, drift with auto-demotion, eval-gated ladder promotion — **together with the four decisions taken 2026-08-20 that made it concrete:** the DIN SPEC 92005 Clause 7 uncertainty-metric spine with its safety-function scope caveat (Decision §5); the ODD-relative coverage route with the *"gewisse Vollständigkeit"* hedge kept verbatim (§6); the Art 5(1)(f) boundary — physical-state operator sensing outside the prohibition, emotions/intentions inference barred by design, re-test on any scope change (item 10, E-2026-08-20-22); and the four-part simulation-credibility requirement (§7, E-2026-08-20-29).

**Minuted with the decision:**

1. **The framework is ratified as strategy; its evidence is not yet in existence.** Eval data sources are the ADR-007 surfaces, which are defined but not built. **R10's qualifier — "eval evidence outstanding" — stands unchanged**, and the board notes expressly that ratifying the strategy moves no requirement and proves no oversight effective.
2. **The hedge is part of the ratified text.** Any future eval claim implying exhaustive coverage exceeds this decision and reopens it.
3. **The Art 5(1)(f) boundary is binding design scope**, on the ADR-003 pattern: drift of any biometric stream toward emotion or intention inference re-runs the test before anything else happens. The GDPR Art 9 test remains unrun and is a precondition of any actual sensing adoption at item 2.
4. Residuals carried as `[I]`: items 1–5, 7, 8, 12–14 (metric builds, adoptions, adjudication-stream instrumentation, in-domain compliance-metric acquisition, data sourcing from ADR-007).

## Resolution 2 — ADR-011 (migration change-control policy): **ratified**

**Status → `Accepted (ARB 2026-08-20/2) — settled internally; no external validation sought or available`**

**Decision ratified:** the five binding rules for change to live bearer/core elements during the parallel run — classification, inactive-redundancy-only, maintenance window, pre-change test gate, blast-radius limit with rehearsed rollback — **together with the scheme artefact written and completed 2026-08-20:** Steps 1–4 (class by blast radius × safety-criticality; CSM-RA Art 4(2) determination with per-class presumptions; SUBSET-146 estate-asymmetry check; cumulative gate matrix), the **NeGSt AUM as Step 2's named scoring reference** with additionality kept outside the point sum (E-2026-08-20-27), and **Step 5 — the parallel authorisation-trigger check** across the EU vehicle layer (Reg 2018/545 / Dir 2016/797 Art 21(12)) and the national EIGV Anlage-4 layer, never inferred from the CSM-RA outcome (E-2026-08-20-28).

**Minuted with the decision:**

1. **The board adopts the item-6 determination as part of the ratified position:** an FRMCS retrofit is not categorically authorisation-triggering; the two-stage pattern splits along the legal line (stage 1 engineerable below both thresholds; stage 2 concentrates the trigger, once per type); trackside FRMCS builds trigger nationally with the § 21 ten-week notification clock, which the gate cadence (item 5) must absorb.
2. **Two named residuals travel with the ratification, unclosed:** the ch.-7 delta of Reg (EU) 2026/693 is checked before any external use of Step 5; and the AUM is cited as NeGSt-2013-as-applied-2026, its point values checked against the current CSM-RA text before external use.
3. **The worked sector examples are corroboration, not validation:** the BR 430 run (E-2026-08-20-26) confirms the process shape in practice; it does not evidence this policy's own operation, which has never been executed.
4. Residuals carried as `[I]`: items 2–5 (codification, gate tie-ins, entry conditions, cadence reconciliation incl. the ten-week window). The PR12 risk-register link and the FRMCS-core extension (PR11) carry forward as implementation under §To revisit.
