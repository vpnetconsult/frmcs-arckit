## Resolution 1 — ADR-012 (cybersecurity conformance): four amendments to a ratified decision, **ratified**

ADR-012 is `Accepted (ARB-2026-08-20 R2)`. It was amended four times across 2026-09-04 without board authorisation. The board adopts the text as written, with one substantive correction already applied before this minute:

1. **CER block upgraded to primary** (E-2026-09-04-02, Dir (EU) 2022/2557 read at source). The Annex names **Rail** as sub-sector 2(b) with **infrastructure managers** (Dir 2012/34/EU Art 3(2)), **railway undertakings** (Art 3(1)) and **operators of service facilities** (Art 3(12)) as entity categories. Art 6(1) identification by **17 July 2026**; transposition due **17 October 2024**, applied from **18 October 2024**; Dir 2008/114/EC repealed same date. **The "directive text not on file" gap closes.**
2. **German transposition identified** (E-2026-09-04-06): **KRITIS-Dachgesetz vom 11. März 2026**, BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 66. ⚠️ **Rail is not named in it** — § 4(1) stops at "Transport und Verkehr", with critical services set by a **BMI Rechtsverordnung (§ 4(3))** and thresholds by another (**§ 5(1)**). The residual therefore **re-points rather than closes**: no rail-specific German CER obligation may be asserted until those Rechtsverordnungen are read. **The board records this as the third instance of one pattern** — RED delegated act, NACSA tatacara, now KRITISDachG: *the statute names the regime, a subordinate instrument names the addressee.* Treat it as a standing acquisition heuristic, not as a finding about any one regime.
3. **A second reporting clock is carried into item 4's SMS work.** § 18 KRITISDachG: report to the **BBK** via the joint BSI/BBK Meldestelle, *unverzüglich* and **within 24 hours**, detailed report **within one month of knowledge**. § 32 BSIG runs its month **from the 72-hour Meldung**. § 18(1) states expressly that other reporting duties remain unaffected — **CER reporting is additive to NIS-2, not absorbed.** An SMS timeline built on a single "one month" assumption breaches one of the two.
4. **Item 4's acquire-and-read instruction is discharged**, all three CSA2 package documents having been read: SWD(2026) 12 (E-2026-08-31-01), **COM(2026) 11** (E-2026-09-04-03), **SWD(2026) 11** (E-2026-09-04-05).

**Correction the board notes rather than smooths over.** The 2026-09-04-03 row initially recorded that the preferred supply-chain framework "contains the exclusion mechanics", correcting an earlier reading taken from the executive summary. On reading the full impact assessment the same day, that was **one turn too blunt** and was annotated in place: option D.3 is an **enabling** regime — the IA states it "would not include directly applicable measures", only empowerments exercisable after a coordinated risk assessment *and* an economic-impact assessment weighing "the availability of alternative suppliers" — while **Title IV Chapter II is the directly-applicable carve-out** for electronic communications networks. **The board adopts the corrected formulation and the test that came with it: score this instrument on USE, not on adoption**, the Commission's own caveat being that de-risking depends on whether the framework is ever used.

## Resolution 2 — ADR-014 (two-plane governance): residual closure and two defect corrections, **ratified**

ADR-014 is `Accepted (ARB-2026-08-23 R2)`, ratified **with binding condition 1**: the NACSA half was a *designation, not a verified position*, and the ADR was to be reopened rather than bent if the mandate did not match.

**(a) Carried action 2's residual is closed, and condition 1 is now substantially discharged on its own terms.** P.U. (A) 220/2024 (gazetted 22.08.2024, in force 26.08.2024) is read at source (E-2026-09-04-08). The s. 23 periods: **reg 2(1)** notify immediately by electronic means on the incident coming to the entity's knowledge; **reg 2(2)** prescribed particulars **within six hours of knowledge**; **reg 2(3)** supplementary information **within fourteen days of the reg 2(1) notification**; **reg 3** via **NC4**, or as the Chief Executive determines if NC4 is disrupted. **The standing prohibition on quoting Malaysian reporting deadlines is LIFTED for reg 2 of P.U. (A) 220/2024 only**, and only where the artifact **states the triggers as well as the durations**. P.U. (A) 219/221/222 remain unread; NCII designation under s. 18 is a separate act, so nothing establishes that any given entity is in scope. **Decision 3, bound (i) is amended accordingly; bound (ii) — criminal rather than administrative enforcement — stands unchanged.**

**(b) ⚠️ The board strikes an overclaim it was asked to ratify.** The closure text asserted that the differing anchors in Malaysian and German law showed "the same failure mode in both planes" and recorded it as a **two-plane finding**. **That is not sustained.** Two instances of ordinary legislative drafting do not evidence a structural property of the two-plane model; every statutory period must pick a trigger, so the observation is unremarkable in any pair of regimes. **It is demoted to a practice note** — *when transcribing a statutory period into an artifact, record its trigger event, never the duration alone* — which is the only part that earns its place. **The board notes the general risk this illustrates: the register's own drafting voice tends to inflate a careful reading into a finding, and that tendency is itself a decision-quality risk, since an overclaim in a ratified ADR is exactly the drift the register exists to catch.**

**(c) A self-contradiction is removed.** Carried action 2 simultaneously stated "STILL NOT ESTABLISHED — THE DEADLINE" and, four lines later, closed the residual. It is collapsed to its closed state with a four-step trail; the superseded stratigraphy remains in the evidence log, which is where it belongs.

**(d) Carried action 3 gains its inheritance:** the Track B stakeholder map, when built, takes the NACSA Chief Executive + **NCII sector lead** as the s. 23 terminal, **Transportation as NCII sector 3**, **NC4** as the channel, and the **s. 11(3) authorised-person structure** (3 per entity, 1 management + 2 working-level) as the operator-side role — established at primary, not to be re-derived.

## Resolution 3 — the retrieval-route error is recorded, not smoothed

In the course of the 2026-09-04 sitting the assistant **constructed the hostname `federalgazette.agc.gov.my` without verifying it existed**, recorded it in an evidence row as a network-policy block, and **requested two egress-policy changes against it**. Neither it nor its `www.` variant resolves at all. The document was ultimately retrieved with **no new rule**, by following links from an already-reachable authority's own page (`nacsa.gov.my/act854.php` → AGC `act-detail` → `lom.agc.gov.my`).

**The board records this because the register's value is that it is honest about its own defects.** Two standing rules are adopted: **(i) verify that a host resolves before requesting an egress rule for it**; **(ii) prefer following links from a reachable authority's own pages over constructing URLs.** The offending instruction in E-2026-09-04-07 is struck through in place with the correction, so no future reader repeats it.
