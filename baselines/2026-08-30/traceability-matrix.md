## Reading notes

1. **Status is the honesty.** R1–R2 and R5–R7 are settled in ADR-001. R3–R4 were nominally covered ("availability — strong by design") but the 23–24 June outage promoted them from assumed to load-bearing-open — they are now things a safety case must actively prove, not assert. R9–R12 are a proposed extension (ADR-002), not yet decided; R13 (fleet-migration feasibility, external dependency) and R14 (cybersecurity — CRA/NIS-2) were added later as adjacent constraints on the transition.
2. **R3 and R4 trace to the incident.** The DB GSM-R outage is not an anecdote; it is the validation evidence for two specific requirements, each with a named remediation decision.
3. **R9–R11 are where the arcKit lens bites on autonomy.** Each bounds the AI rather than empowering it: a separate layer (R9), proportional oversight (R10), a certified core left untouched (R11). That is how "agentic" survives contact with a SIL-4 safety authority.
4. **Evidence gaps are explicit, not silent.** R9 carries no logged external evidence (proposal-only); R11's boundary is now **designed** in ADR-004 (unidirectional boundary, human-in-command, EN 50129 freedom-from-interference) and now has **design-basis** external evidence — the RCA/OCORA + DB/Siemens "SIL4 Data Center" Safe-Computing-Platform reference (E-2026-07-02-01), extended by the DSD "SIL4 Cloud" / separation-kernel report (E-2026-07-02-03) — but the **verifying** safety-case evidence (independence/FFI analysis, ISA/NoBo) is still to be produced. R11 rests on CENELEC EN 5012x and *underpins* the ADR-003 / E-2026-06-24-07 EU AI Act classification (the "not high-risk" finding holds only while the certified-kernel/no-actuation boundary holds), so the classification is only as firm as R11. These are marked in the evidence-ref column rather than left blank. Trust tiers in evidence-log.md were also corrected for over-confidence (E-01 A/C→C; E-03/E-04 A-weight scoped to genuinely primary sub-sources).

## Status legend

- **Accepted** — decision made and recorded in an ADR, mechanism defined. (Formal ARB/NSA ratification is tracked separately via the ADR's own status — e.g. ADR-001 is Accepted at requirement level but still Proposed pending ratification.)
- **Strengthened post-incident** — previously assumed; the 23–24 Jun outage raised it to a must-prove item.
- **Open** — decision direction set; design/validation outstanding.
- **Proposed** — not yet decided; carried in ADR-002.
- **Recommended** — policy-level position, decision sits above the architecture team.
